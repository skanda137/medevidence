Redesign the entire frontend of this application into a world-class medical AI platform used by physicians, hospitals, and healthcare professionals.

The design should feel premium, modern, trustworthy, elegant, and highly professional—not futuristic, flashy, or consumer-oriented.

Think of the design language as a combination of:

• Apple Human Interface Guidelines
• Stripe Dashboard
• Linear.app
• Notion
• Vercel
• Osmosis
• OpenEvidence
• UpToDate (modernized)
• Glass Health

The application should use a LIGHT THEME with excellent readability.

---------------------------------------------------
DESIGN PRINCIPLES
---------------------------------------------------

Doctors spend hours reading clinical content.

Prioritize:

• extremely clean layout
• lots of whitespace
• calm interface
• visual hierarchy
• minimal distractions
• accessibility
• readability
• fast information scanning

The interface should inspire confidence and trust.

Avoid:

Dark theme
Heavy gradients
Neon colors
Cyberpunk effects
Oversized typography
Rounded cartoon buttons
Glassmorphism
Over-animations
Visual clutter
